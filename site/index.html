How to read this

  • Darker means higher. Districts are sorted into five groups of equal size, so each shade holds a fifth of the state's districts.
  • The margin of error is how far the true number could plausibly be from the estimate. A ±1.1 point margin on 10.3% means the real figure is probably between 9.2% and 11.4%. Small shares in small districts can have large margins, and the page marks those as shaky.
  • These are 5-year averages (2020 to 2024), not a snapshot of today.
  • Electric heating is a rough sign, not a measure of clean energy. The Census doesn't count clean energy by district. It does count how homes are heated, which says something about who depends on the power bill for warmth.
  • The page shows what the numbers say. It doesn't explain why a district looks the way it does.
